# Malaysia Personal Tax Calculator

A comprehensive, interactive web-based calculator for computing Malaysia Personal Income Tax based on the Malaysia Tax Act.

## 🇲🇾 Features

- **Income Calculation**: Calculate annual tax based on monthly gross salary and other income
- **Personal Reliefs**: Support for all major tax reliefs including:
  - Individual and dependent relatives relief
  - Children reliefs (unmarried, in education)
  - Medical and health expenses
  - Education and self-enhancement
  - Insurance and retirement savings
  - Family and childcare expenses
  - And many more...

- **PCB Management**: Optional Monthly Tax Deduction (PCB) tracking and credit
- **Progressive Tax Brackets**: Based on Malaysia Tax Act rates (YA 2024 & 2025)
- **Real-time Calculations**: Results update instantly as you enter data
- **Beautiful UI**: Modern, responsive design with smooth animations
- **No Data Collection**: All calculations are done locally in your browser

## 📊 Tax Calculation

The calculator uses the official Malaysia tax brackets:
- 0% on first RM 5,000
- 1% on RM 5,001 - RM 20,000
- 3% on RM 20,001 - RM 35,000
- 6% on RM 35,001 - RM 50,000
- 11% on RM 50,001 - RM 70,000
- 19% on RM 70,001 - RM 100,000
- 25% on RM 100,001 - RM 400,000
- 26% on RM 400,001 - RM 600,000
- 28% on RM 600,001 - RM 2,000,000
- 30% on above RM 2,000,000

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Enter your monthly gross salary
2. Add any other annual income (bonus, rental, dividends, etc.)
3. Select applicable tax reliefs and enter amounts
4. Optionally add PCB deduction information
5. Click "Calculate Tax" to see results
6. View your final tax payable and effective tax rate

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This calculator is a prototype for estimation purposes only. It is based on Malaysia Tax Act regulations for YA 2024 and 2025. For accurate tax calculations, please consult with a tax professional or refer to official LHDN (Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri Malaysia) guidelines. This calculator does not replace official tax filing requirements.
